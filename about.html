About

Practical by background

I came to software after thirteen years in warehouse operations. That's where I learned that a system is only as good as how it behaves on a busy day, when the data is messy and people are relying on it.

I went back to school for computer science and graduated from Colorado Technical University in 2024, summa cum laude, with a concentration in software engineering. While studying, I joined Coding Dojo, first as a teaching assistant and then as an instructor, teaching full-stack web development for about three years.

Since May 2025 I've been a contract software engineer, working hands-on in production. I build and maintain business applications that cross React frontends, NestJS and .NET APIs, third-party integrations, and SQL databases. That includes scheduling and calendar sync, estimate workflows, data integration, and the debugging that keeps it all running.

Teaching shows up in how I work. I explain tradeoffs in plain language, write down what I find, and try to leave code and documentation that the next person can pick up without me.
